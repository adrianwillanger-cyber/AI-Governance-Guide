A FEW PRACTICAL DETAILS

Before you get started.

What do I receive?

The Building an AI Governance Program guide and AI Use Policy fillable template, with an intake worksheet and employee checklist. Delivery is through Gumroad after purchase. Review the current Gumroad listing for the edition and full file list before buying.

Is the policy PDF actually fillable?

Yes. The policy template has nine fillable fields, including linked company-name fields. The intake worksheet and employee checklist are supporting resources, not additional interactive forms or an automated policy generator. Completing the fields personalizes the draft; it does not replace substantive review.

Which PDF viewer should I use?

Use a PDF reader that supports interactive forms. Behavior can vary across viewers, especially lightweight mobile apps. Save a filled copy, reopen it, and inspect print preview to confirm that every entry is visible. Shorten or split long entries as needed, and keep the original blank for reuse.

Will this reduce my legal bill?

No specific saving is promised. The purpose is to help you organize your business decisions and prepare a draft for review, rather than begin with a blank page. The legal work required will depend on your business, jurisdiction, and proposed uses; counsel may recommend substantial changes.

Does this replace legal advice?

No. This is educational material and a working draft, not legal advice, legal-review services or a compliance certification. Have qualified counsel review your policy before adopting it, especially for hiring, lending, healthcare or other regulated decisions.
