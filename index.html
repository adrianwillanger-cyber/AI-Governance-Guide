A FEW PRACTICAL DETAILS

Before you get started.

What do I receive?

Two digital PDFs: the 41-page Building an AI Governance Program guide and the AI Use Policy fillable template. The bundle includes an intake worksheet and an employee checklist. Delivery is through Gumroad after purchase.

Is the policy PDF actually fillable?

Yes. The policy template has nine fillable fields, including linked company-name fields. The intake worksheet and employee checklist are supporting resources, not an automated policy generator. Preview your completed PDF before sharing it.

Which PDF viewer should I use?

Adobe Acrobat Reader is the recommended starting point. Form behavior can vary across PDF viewers, especially lightweight mobile apps. Save a filled copy and keep the original blank for reuse.

Does this replace legal advice?

No. This is educational material and a working draft, not legal advice, legal-review services or a compliance certification. Have qualified counsel review your policy before adopting it, especially for hiring, lending, healthcare or other regulated decisions.
